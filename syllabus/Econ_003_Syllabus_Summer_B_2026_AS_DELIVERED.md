## 2. Course description

Introduces the fundamental principles of microeconomics, focusing on individual and firm
decision-making and how those decisions shape market outcomes and the allocation of resources.
Topics: supply and demand, consumer behavior, production and costs, market structure, international
trade, externalities and public goods, and government intervention in cases of market failure.
Students apply economic reasoning to real-world questions involving pricing, taxation and public
policy.

## 3. Required materials

**Final-replaces-midterm.** If the final exceeds the midterm, the final score replaces the midterm
and the final effectively counts 65%. One-directional only: the midterm never replaces the final.
